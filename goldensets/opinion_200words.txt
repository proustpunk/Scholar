I believe the best diet is one rich in whole foods and low in processed ingredients. Processed foods contain too much sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats. They cause inflammation and chronic disease. Whole foods provide essential nutrients and fiber. The Mediterranean diet is widely considered the healthiest. It emphasizes olive oil, fish, vegetables, and fruits. Red meat should be limited. Dairy is optional. Eggs are nutritious despite past cholesterol concerns. Intermittent fasting might help with weight control. Plant-based diets are better for the environment. Keto can work short-term but is unsustainable. Everyone's body responds differently. There's no perfect diet for all people. Listen to your body. Eat mindfully. Cook at home. Read labels. Avoid artificial ingredients. Moderation is key.
