These concepts build sequentially into a critique of structural logic, arguing that poor organization leads to conceptual errors. **The core tension is between effective explanatory structures and flawed procedural formatting that obscures meaning.**

**Layer 2: Per-Concept Breakdown**

**Critique of Procedural Texts**
Procedural texts are criticized for presenting data as if it explains a concept that is already assumed to be known. This creates a structural mismatch where the format fails to support the intended pedagogical goal. **Without an essay format, this structure is inherently prone to error.**

**Opinions and Contradictions**
The text argues that opinions are poorly managed and contradictions are erroneously flattened into single ideas. This conflation prevents clear distinction between conflicting viewpoints or data points. **Unlike the structural critique, this focuses on the content’s internal logic failing to handle complexity.**

**Analogy Structure Failure**
The "sticky analogy" is cited as a bad structural choice because it does not properly separate concept from data. The proposed correction involves using analogies strictly for concepts while using summaries for data. **This contrasts with the current flawed method by enforcing a clearer boundary between conceptual understanding and factual reporting.**

**Layer 3: The Synthesis Crib Sheet** (technical accuracy + integration)
- **Structural Rule:** Procedural text ≠ Concept explanation (data presentation assumes known concepts).
- **Content Rule:** Do not conflate contradictions; handle opinions distinctly.
- **Revised Structure:** Analogy → Concept | Summary → Data.

| Flawed Element | Proposed Correction |
| :--- | :--- |
| Procedural text explaining concepts | Essay format or assuming concepts are known |
| Conflating contradictions | Keep contradictions distinct; handle opinions separately |
| "Sticky" analogy for everything | Analogy for concept; Summary for data |

**Logical Chain:** The current procedural structure fails because it mishandles opinions and conflates contradictions. To fix this, the text suggests a specific structural separation: map analogies to concepts and summaries to data.

**Gotcha Points:**
Professors may quiz on why procedural texts are "bound to be wrong" if not essays; remember this refers to the **assumption that concepts are already known**. Students must not confuse "analogy for concept" with "summary for data," as mixing these is the specific structural failure identified as the "sticky analogy."
