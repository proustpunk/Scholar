    *   *Implication:* A "best" diet is a trade-off. One might choose a plant-based diet for environmental reasons even if they personally thrive on eggs/dairy, or choose Keto for short-term breakdown despite long-term sustainability issues.

4.  **Resolution of Historical Conflicts**
    *   *Insight:* The text updates outdated nutritional dogma (e.g., cholesterol from eggs) while maintaining strict views on processed ingredients.
    *   *Implication:* The distinction between "natural/whole" and "artificial/processed" is more critical than traditional macro-nutrient fears (like cholesterol). The focus is on the *source* and *processing* of food rather than isolated nutrients.
