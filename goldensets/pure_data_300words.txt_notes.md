**Professor "Gotcha" Points**
1. **Don't confuse Gross Margin with Net Margin:** A professor might ask for the "bottom line" percentage; ensure you cite 17% (Net) rather than 62% (Gross).
2. **Calculate LTV/CAC Ratio manually:** While the text gives both numbers, exams often require calculating the ratio (7.0) to prove you understand the significance of the relationship, not just the raw values.
